YOUR PERSONALIZED BIRTHDAY WEBSITE 💗

Your six uploaded photos are already included and named correctly.

To preview:
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the birthday_surprise_with_photos folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome.

To publish on GitHub Pages:
Upload index.html and all six photo files (photo1.jpg to photo6.jpg) into the root of your repository.
Then enable Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root).

Countdown note:
The page counts down to the next midnight according to the visitor's device time. Keep the page open to see it unlock automatically.
